Introduction

Traditional antibiotics often fail, especially against multidrug-resistant (MDR) bacteria. Bacteriophages (phages), discovered in 1919, provide a promising therapeutic opportunity but have seen limited use due to a lack of investment in phage standardization and regulatory frameworks. This research seeks to explore phage therapy as a viable alternative or complement to conventional antibiotics, aiming to establish standardized testing methodologies for clinical application. By harnessing artificial intelligence to create personalized combinations of phages, specifically built to patients needs, the chances of resistance developing drops dramatically.
